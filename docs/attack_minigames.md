# Attack minigames, per enemy and per attack

This is a design table for the next step of *EarthBound - Bullet Hell*: instead of every
enemy action being "dodge box three times in four, timed block otherwise", each
(enemy, action) pair gets its own three-to-five-second minigame that reads as that
attack. The table was built from `ebsrc/src/data/battle/enemies.asm` (every enemy record's
four `BATTLE_ACTIONS` plus its final action) and `ebsrc/src/data/battle/action_table.asm`
(what each action is: physical, piercing physical, PSI, item or "other", and what its
callback does). Rows are grouped by enemy in the game's order; enemies that have several
records (the second form after an `ENEMY_EXTENDER`, the four Giygas phases, the three
Clumsy Robots) are one group with all their record numbers listed and each attack once.

What is skipped: `NO_EFFECT` ("Nothing"), `ENEMY_EXTENDER` (a record switch, not an
attack), the scripted events (`RUNAWAY_FIVE_EVENT`, `MASTER_BARF_DEFEAT`, `POKEY_SPEECH_*`,
`GIVE_OFF_RAINBOW`) and the pure-flavour actions whose callback is `BTLACT_NULL*` and that
change nothing (Grin, Howl, Knit Brow, Edge Closer, On Guard, Wobble, Reel, Tick-tock,
Whisper/Murmur/Mutter, Flash Smile, Laugh Hysterically, Fall Down, Be Absentminded, Take
Deep Breath, Greet, Generate Steam, Bark, Chant Spell, Scratch Head, the Clumsy Robot
chores, Wolfed Down Fly Honey, Be Pokey and the Giygas quotes). They are listed at the end
so nothing is lost; they could become one-second "tells" for the next real attack. Enemies
with no real action at all (Pokey, Teddy Bear, Super Plush Bear, Magic Butterfly) get no
rows.

Every minigame is meant to fit the existing engine: a game window (up to 232 x 88 px),
the heart, up to 32 bullets cut from the enemy's own sprite, the grade labels
(PERFECT / GOOD / OK / MISS), a one-line hint along the bottom edge, and a D-pad plus
A/B/X/Y (L/R are free). A PERFECT dodges or cancels the action, GOOD/OK scale the damage
or the status chance (50 % / 75 %), MISS is the full effect, exactly as the timed block
does today. Heal, buff and summon actions are "interrupt" games: a good grade shrinks the
heal, weakens the buff or stops the reinforcement, a MISS lets it through in full.

## Building blocks

The concepts below are composed from these reusable mechanics. B1 and B2 exist already;
the others are the small set the engine would need to add. Each is a self-contained
update-and-render routine that reads the same heart position, bullet table and grade code.

1. **B1 Dodge box** - the heart moves in the box (B or X to move slowly) while bullets
   enter from the edges; blue bullets only hurt a moving heart, orange only a still one.
2. **B2 Bracket press** - four brackets close on a target; press A when they frame it,
   graded by pixel distance (the timed block / focus game).
3. **B3 Danger cells** - the interior is a grid of cells; cells flash a warning for a
   few frames, then hurt for a moment; the safe cells stay dark. Patterns are data.
4. **B4 Sweeps** - a full-width or full-height bar with a gap crosses the box; the
   heart must be in the gap when it passes. Speed, gap size and direction are data.
5. **B5 Hold and release** - hold a button while a meter fills; release inside the
   marked window. Too early or too late is a MISS.
6. **B6 Arrow chain** - three to six D-pad arrows appear in a row; input them in order
   before the timer bar empties (the combo game with directions instead of face buttons).
7. **B7 Scrambled controls** - the D-pad is mirrored, rotated or lagged for the length
   of the game (a status-flavour twist on B1).
8. **B8 Pull** - a constant force drags the heart toward a point or an edge; the player
   must push against it and stay off the harmful side.
9. **B9 Closing walls** - one or more box edges move inward over the game; survive in
   the shrinking space until the timer runs out.
10. **B10 Catch and avoid** - things drift through the box; touch the good ones (they
    reduce the effect) and avoid the bad ones. Uses the bullet table with a "good" flag.
11. **B11 Spotlight** - the interior is dark except a circle that follows the heart (or
    the bullets); everything else is invisible until it enters the circle.
12. **B12 Wiggle** - alternate Left/Right (or two buttons) as fast as possible to fill a
    meter before it drains; the grade is the fill when time is up.
13. **B13 Counter shot** - a crosshair in the box; the enemy's cut-out sprite piece
    crosses it and A fires; a centred hit knocks the attack back (PERFECT).
14. **B14 Beat** - a metronome ticks at the enemy's tempo (the tempo byte in
    `bh_enemies.asm`); press on the tick, off-beat presses count as hits.
15. **B15 Face it** - a tell shows which side the attack comes from; hold the D-pad in
    that direction when it lands (the heart turns into a small shield facing that way).
16. **B16 Pick a cell** - three to five cells; one is safe; a tell reveals it briefly
    and the heart must be inside it at the reveal.
17. **B17 Freeze** - a window in which the heart must not move (or no button may be
    pressed); moving during it is a hit. The orange-bullet rule as a whole-box state.
18. **B18 Copy** - the enemy shows a short sequence (coloured cells, arrows, shapes);
    reproduce it after it disappears.
19. **B19 Count steps** - each press moves the heart one fixed step; land exactly on
    the marked spot with the right number of presses.
20. **B20 Two hearts** - a second, mirrored heart follows the first (mirrored left/right
    or up/down); both must stay clear of the bullets.
